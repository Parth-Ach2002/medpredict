About MedPredict

System information, methodology, and model details.

🔬 Methodology

MedPredict uses an ensemble approach combining Bagging (Random Forest) and Boosting (XGBoost) classifiers. The best-performing model is selected automatically based on cross-validated AUC score.

Uncertainty estimation is performed using Monte Carlo Dropout with T=50 stochastic forward passes, producing a mean prediction and standard deviation (σ).

When σ > 0.10, a Doctor Review Flag is raised to defer the decision to clinical staff.
